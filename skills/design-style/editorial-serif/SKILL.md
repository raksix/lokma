# Editorial Serif

Long-form reading with a printed-page feel. Choose this when the content is
prose and the reader is expected to stay.

## Type

- Serif display face for headings (Georgia/`Times New Roman` stack is fine),
  sans-serif for UI chrome only.
- Body at 18-20px with `1.65` line-height and a measure of 60-70 characters
  (`max-width: 68ch`).
- Drop caps or a pull quote for the first screen; nothing else needs one.

## Surfaces

- Warm paper background (`#FFFBF5`-ish) with pure white cards, or the reverse.
- Hairline rules (`1px`, low-contrast) instead of heavy dividers.
- Generous vertical rhythm: sections separated by space, not by boxes.

## Colour and motion

- One ink colour plus a single accent for links and bylines.
- Motion stays out of the reading column; only hover states may transition.

## Pitfalls

- Centring body copy destroys the measure.
- More than two font families turns a magazine into a poster.
