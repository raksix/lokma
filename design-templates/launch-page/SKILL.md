# Launch Page — one conversion column

A single scrolling page, `max-width` content measure around 68ch with wider
full-bleed bands for hero and final CTA. The reader's question at every scroll
position is "is this for me?".

## Section order

1. **Nav** — product name, three anchor links, one primary button. Sticky, thin.
2. **Hero** — the promise in one headline sentence, one line of mechanics under
   it, one primary and one secondary action. No hero image: build interest with
   type scale and space, or with a live-looking product frame.
3. **Proof strip** — logos, install counts or numbers, as one quiet row. Real
   values from the brief; never a greyed placeholder logo box.
4. **Three capabilities** — what it does, each with a title and two sentences.
   Concrete nouns, not adjectives.
5. **Objections** — the honest list: what it does not do, what it costs, who it
   is not for. This section is what makes the rest believable.
6. **Pricing** — one plan, one number, one period, one included line.
7. **Close** — the same action as the hero, restated for someone who scrolled
   the whole way.

## Rules

- Two buttons, never three, in any section.
- Each section's `<h2>` names its own benefit, not its topic ("Ship in an
  afternoon" beats "Features").
- No `background-image` from the network. Inline SVG only.
- The final CTA is reachable with the keyboard and states its destination in the
  label, not only in colour.
