# Brand Voice

Consistent voice across headlines, buttons and supporting copy.

## Voice

- Pick one register and hold it: plain-confident, warm-conversational or
  precise-clinical. Mixed registers read as careless.
- Address the reader as a person doing a job ("you"), never as a segment
  ("modern teams").

## Headlines

- Lead with the outcome the reader gets, not the feature name.
- 3-9 words. One idea per headline.

## Buttons and microcopy

- Buttons are verbs: "Start free", "Book a demo". Never "Submit", "Click here",
  "Learn more".
- Error copy says what happened and what to do next; it never blames the user.
- No exclamation marks. No emoji in product copy.

## Pitfalls

- Feature-name headlines ("Introducing X 2.0") describe the seller, not the
  reader's outcome.
- Mixing "you" and "users/teams/customers" across a page breaks the voice.
