# Product Hierarchy

A marketing or product screen has one job: make the next step obvious.

## Structure

- Order: value proposition → proof → features → primary CTA. Do not repeat the
  CTA in a different wording; one phrasing, reused verbatim.
- The primary CTA appears above the fold AND after the feature block. Keep
  both identical in label, size and colour.

## Density

- Whitespace carries the hierarchy: 8px grid, sections separated by at least
  48px. Do not add boxes to create grouping where spacing already does it.
- Three to five feature blocks maximum. More becomes an undifferentiated wall.

## Type scale

- H1 at 40-56px desktop / 32-36px mobile, body 16-18px, captions 13-14px.
- Sentence case for headings; avoid ALL CAPS over long strings.

## Pitfalls

- Two competing primary buttons (two filled) leaves the user without a default.
- A hero that needs a scroll to explain the product is a hero that failed.
